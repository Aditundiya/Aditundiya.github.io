Comet Loop Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026

Comet Loop ("the app", "we") is a casual puzzle game. This policy explains how the app handles data.

The short version
  • The game works fully offline. Your progress (levels, stars, settings, skins, streak) is stored only on your device and is never uploaded by us.
  • We do not ask for, collect, or store your name, email, contacts, location, or any account.
  • The app has no ads and no in-app purchases.
  • When your device is online, the app sends anonymous gameplay statistics and crash reports to Google Firebase so we can fix bugs and improve the game. No advertising ID is collected.

Data stored on your device

Game progress, star balance, unlocked skins, daily streak, and settings are saved in the app's local storage on your device only. Uninstalling the app deletes this data. We have no copy of it and no way to access it.

Anonymous statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

When the device has an internet connection, the app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. They receive:

  • gameplay events such as a level being started, completed or quit, the level number, number of taps and time taken;
  • crash reports (the error, device model and Android version);
  • a random app-instance identifier that Firebase generates on install, plus approximate country derived from the IP address.

We do not set a user ID, and the app does not collect the advertising ID. This data is not linked to your name, email or account, and is not used for advertising. It is processed by Google under its privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Notifications

If you allow it, the app schedules daily puzzle reminders on your device. These are created locally; no notification data is sent to us.

Children

Comet Loop is content-appropriate for all ages and rated Everyone, with a target audience of 13 and older. The app does not request any personal information from any user.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new effective date.
